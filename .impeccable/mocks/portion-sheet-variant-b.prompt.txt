Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web app composition reference, brainstorming only
Primary request: Create a full desktop-first viewport (1440x1000) for a Brazilian pet food daily portion calculator. One focused operational screen, no marketing navigation and no photos. The visual world is an intelligent pantry-label calibration sheet: deep ink-blue background, bright warm ivory paper panels, electric vermilion as the deliberate action color, muted seafoam for calm data states, small charcoal typography with tabular numerals. The calculator must feel like an easy kitchen tool, not a veterinary dashboard.
Composition/framing: Variant B. The first viewport is a horizontal calibration strip: a narrow status band at top, a dense four-step input rail across the upper half with connected numbered stations for species, ideal weight, profile, and metabolizable energy, then a broad perforated-paper output ticket revealed below. The output ticket owns the lower half with oversized daily grams on the left and daily calories plus a three-step calculation trail on the right. A concise clinical limit notice is integrated as an edge label, always visible. Use visual geometry inspired by food package nutrition labels and kitchen measuring tapes, but do not include product brands, logos, animal photos, fake claims, or decorative paw prints.
Style/medium: editorial-grade UI mockup, crisp vector-like web interface, very high fidelity.
Text (verbatim): Portuguese only, but keep all text short and legible.
Constraints: no top nav, no hero photo, no stock bento cards, no glassmorphism, no rounded generic cards, no watermark.
