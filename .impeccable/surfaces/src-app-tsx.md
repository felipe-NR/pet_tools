## Job and audience

- **Mode:** Operate. A Brazilian guardian with a food package and kitchen scale needs a trustworthy starting portion for a healthy adult dog or cat.
- **Task:** Fill four familiar inputs, calculate, and read the daily grams with the calculation trace and clinical limits immediately adjacent.

## Chosen direction

- **World:** Pantry-label calibration sheet. An ink-blue work surface frames warm ivory measurement sheets, vermilion marks action and exceptions, and seafoam carries non-critical calculated data.
- **Composition:** The first viewport uses the approved Variant A comp at `.impeccable/mocks/portion-sheet-variant-a.png`: a vertical numbered form sheet on the left and a persistent output ticket on the right; the output has an honest empty state until a result exists.
- **Memorable moment:** Submitting the form turns the quiet output ticket into a large, tabular grams-per-day reading, with the visible formula trail directly beneath it.
- **Responsive rule:** At narrow widths, the form sheet precedes the output ticket without losing its numbered sequence or clinical boundary.

## Boundaries and states

- Keep all product copy, validation, scope warnings, formulas, and accessibility semantics from the existing application. The generated comp is visual-only: its invented values and advice are not product copy.
- Inputs, keyboard focus, field errors, atypical-energy warning, initial result state, calculated state, hover, and reduced-motion behavior must all belong to the same label system.
- The screen stays a single local calculator; no navigation, telemetry, backend, animal images, or fabricated veterinary advice.
